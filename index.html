Privacy Policy — Gita Reels

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Gita Reels is published by Jagdish Choudhary. This policy explains what the app does with data. The short version: the app has no account, no login, and no server of its own.

What stays on your device

  • Your bookmarks and your last-read position. Saved in the app's own private storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes them. If you have Android's backup enabled, Android may include them in your own device backup.
  • Share cards. Tapping share renders a picture of the verse into the app's temporary cache so the app you pick can read it. Android clears that cache on its own.

We do not collect, transmit or store your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, files or any other personal information. There is no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Advertising

The app shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google receives data from your device, which may include your advertising ID, IP address, device type and coarse location derived from the IP address. Google uses this to choose and measure ads.

  • Google's handling of this data: business.safety.google/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ad loads, using Google's consent platform. You can change that choice later: open the saved-verses sheet (the bookmark icon) and tap Privacy options.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Speech

Reading a verse aloud uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone — on most devices that is Google's. The verse text is passed to that engine to be spoken. Its handling of that text is governed by its own provider's privacy policy, not ours.

Children

The app is not directed at children. Because it serves ads, it is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase on request. Data held by Google as the ad provider is covered by Google's own policy, linked above, which includes the controls Google offers you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
